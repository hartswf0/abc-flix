How to use CAUSALITY

B - BUILD: Chat with Claude to develop your story. Say "make a movie idea", "write the script", "break into shots". Claude builds the story structure.

A - SHOTS: Your shot list lives here. Each shot can be rendered to dot-matrix frames via BEFLIX. Tap "RENDER ALL SHOTS" to generate all frames at once.

C - COLOR: Draw directly on the canvas. Pen, box, erase. 8 ink levels. Stamp text onto frames.

D - SAVE: Export WebM, MP4, GIF, PNG, contact sheet, or the full story package.

TIMELINE: Each shot becomes a clip of frames. Tap frames to navigate. SEL mode to select ranges.

BEFLIX: The rendering language. Claude generates animation code per shot; the engine runs it frame-by-frame on the 128x96 dot-matrix grid.

Press SPACE to play/pause. Arrow keys to step. 1-4 for tabs.
